Bùbù recording session 2

260 sentences in 11 batches, then 156 words in 4 batches. About 30–40 minutes in all; it's fine to do it over several days.

Part 1: the sentences

  • Read each one naturally, in your own normal voice, as if saying it to a friend. Don't act the characters (no child's voice for 朵朵, no deep voice for 陈爸爸).
  • A steady, relaxed pace: not slow, not rushed. Questions as questions.
  • Leave about three seconds of silence after each line. Don't say the number.
  • If you make a mistake, pause, say "重来", pause, and read that line again. The one after 重来 is kept.

Part 2: the words

  • Now like a teacher saying a word to a beginner: clearly, a little slowly, with the full tones. A third tone on its own should dip and rise all the way (你, 好, 我).
  • Say each word once, then about two seconds of silence.
  • Same "重来" rule for mistakes.

The room and the phone

  • A quiet room with soft things around: a wardrobe full of clothes, or a bedroom with curtains shut. No kitchen or bathroom (echo). No music or TV.
  • Phone about 20 cm from the mouth, a little to one side so breath doesn't hit it. The same place every batch.
  • Voice Memos is fine. Turn off anything that "enhances" the sound.
  • Before the rest, record batch S01 only and send it over, so we can check the sound first.

Saving

One recording per batch, named as shown: s01, s02 … and w01, w02 …. Any format the phone makes is fine. Put them in chineseLearning\audio\session2.
