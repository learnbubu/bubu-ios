Bùbù recording script

__COUNT__ syllables in __BATCHES__ batches of up to 40. Each takes about five minutes.

How to say each one

  • Say the syllable once, the way a teacher says a single character to a beginner: clearly, a little slowly, with the full tone. A third tone should dip and rise all the way.
  • Read the pinyin, not the character, if the character has more than one reading.
  • Leave about two seconds of silence after each one. Don't say the number.
  • If you make a mistake, pause, say "重来", pause, and say that line again. The one after 重来 is kept.

The room and the phone

  • A quiet room with soft things around: a wardrobe full of clothes, or a bedroom with curtains shut. No kitchen or bathroom (echo).
  • Phone about 20 cm from the mouth, a little to one side so breath doesn't hit it. The same place every session.
  • Voice Memos is fine. Turn off anything that "enhances" the sound.
  • Before the first batch, record lines 1 to 10 only and send them over. We'll check the sound before doing the rest.

Saving

One recording per batch, named as shown: batch01, batch02 and so on. Any format the phone makes is fine.
