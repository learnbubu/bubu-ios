# Bùbù's voices: what each person records

Each character's voice is copied from one short Mandarin sample (CosyVoice 3, `tools/clone/`).
So each person records about 15 seconds, not every line. Bùbù herself is the owner's partner.

## How to record (the same for everyone)

- A quiet room with soft things around: a bedroom, or a wardrobe of clothes. No kitchen or
  bathroom (echo), and no music or TV.
- The phone about 20 cm away, angled a little to the side so breath doesn't pop the mic.
  Voice Memos is fine; switch off anything that "enhances" the sound.
- Read your lines **two or three times** in one recording, with a pause between takes.
- Speak as the character would to a friend: natural, not reading aloud.
- Say the words exactly as written. If you change one, tell us which.
- Send the file as it is, named after the character (e.g. `chenming.m4a`).

## The scripts

Each is in the character's own voice and about 15 seconds read naturally.

### 马克 Mark: a man, late 20s to 30s
A Londoner learning Chinese, friendly and a bit disorganised. A native English speaker with good
Mandarin is ideal (he's the learner, so a light accent suits him); a native speaker is fine too.

> 你好！我叫马克，我是英国人，住在伦敦。我在学中文，每个星期六都去唐人街练习。说得不好，可是我很喜欢！

Nǐ hǎo! Wǒ jiào Mǎkè, wǒ shì Yīngguó rén, zhù zài Lúndūn. Wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén, měi gè xīngqīliù dōu qù Tángrénjiē liànxí. Shuō de bù hǎo, kěshì wǒ hěn xǐhuan!

### 陈明 Chen Ming: a man, early 30s, native (Beijing ideally)
The chef at Chen Family Dumplings: warm, teasing, always busy.

> 来来来，饺子刚做好，快趁热吃！今天饭馆特别忙，我从早上六点忙到现在。不过没关系，看你们吃得这么香，我就高兴了。

Lái lái lái, jiǎozi gāng zuò hǎo, kuài chèn rè chī! Jīntiān fànguǎn tèbié máng, wǒ cóng zǎoshang liù diǎn máng dào xiànzài. Búguò méi guānxi, kàn nǐmen chī de zhème xiāng, wǒ jiù gāoxìng le.

### 陈妈妈 Chen Ming's mum: a woman, 50s to 60s, native
Runs the front of the restaurant and feeds everyone. Warm, fussy, motherly.

> 哎呀，你怎么又瘦了？多吃点儿，多吃点儿！这是我自己包的饺子，还有汤。天冷了，出门要多穿件衣服，别感冒了。

Āiyā, nǐ zěnme yòu shòu le? Duō chī diǎnr, duō chī diǎnr! Zhè shì wǒ zìjǐ bāo de jiǎozi, hái yǒu tāng. Tiān lěng le, chūmén yào duō chuān jiàn yīfu, bié gǎnmào le.

### 陈爸爸 Chen Ming's dad: a man, 60s, native
Retired, loves Beijing opera and his flask of tea. Relaxed and a little slow.

> 我退休以后啊，每天就喝喝茶，听听京剧，带孙女去公园走走。年纪大了，腿不太好，可是心情好，比什么都重要。

Wǒ tuìxiū yǐhòu a, měitiān jiù hē he chá, tīng ting jīngjù, dài sūnnǚ qù gōngyuán zǒu zou. Niánjì dà le, tuǐ bú tài hǎo, kěshì xīnqíng hǎo, bǐ shénme dōu zhòngyào.

### 朵朵 Duoduo: a girl of about 8, native
Chen Ming's daughter, born in London. Bright, cheeky, full of questions.

> 你好！我叫朵朵，我八岁了。我会说中文，也会说英语。马克，你为什么说中文这么慢啊？我教你吧！

Nǐ hǎo! Wǒ jiào Duǒduo, wǒ bā suì le. Wǒ huì shuō Zhōngwén, yě huì shuō Yīngyǔ. Mǎkè, nǐ wèishénme shuō Zhōngwén zhème màn a? Wǒ jiāo nǐ ba!

(For a child: a parent or guardian must agree in writing, and should be there while she records.)

### 林小雨 Xiaoyu: a woman, early 20s, native (Sichuan/Chengdu ideally)
Only if Xiaoyu should sound different from Bùbù. For now both use the owner's partner.

> 我是成都人，现在在伦敦学设计。我喜欢音乐，也喜欢唱歌。周末有空的话，我们一起去喝奶茶吧！

Wǒ shì Chéngdū rén, xiànzài zài Lúndūn xué shèjì. Wǒ xǐhuan yīnyuè, yě xǐhuan chànggē. Zhōumò yǒu kòng de huà, wǒmen yìqǐ qù hē nǎichá ba!

## The agreement (get it in writing from everyone)

A short note, signed (a message or an email is fine), saying:

- Their recordings will be used to make a computer copy of their voice for the characters in the
  app Bùbù, which may be sold.
- The copy will only speak the app's learning content, as that character.
- They can ask for their voice to be taken out of the app at any time, and it will be replaced.
- (For a child: signed by a parent or guardian.)

If someone is paid (Fiverr, Voices.com), say up front that it's for **AI voice cloning in a
commercial app**. A plain voice-over licence doesn't cover it.
